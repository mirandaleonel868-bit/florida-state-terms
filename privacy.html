Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, staff ranks, moderation records, ticket information, and session activity when necessary to provide its features.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide staff management, promotions, infractions, tickets, sessions, voting, and audit logs.

3. Data Storage

Information may be stored in the bot's database. Access should be restricted to authorized personnel.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be visible to authorized server staff when required for moderation and management features.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the bot's functionality and legitimate server management purposes. For data-related requests, contact the bot's owner.

6. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect stored information, although no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The bot is intended to operate within Discord's applicable rules and age requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the bot's features or data practices change.

9. Contact

Contact the Florida State Management team through its official support channel for privacy questions or requests.

10. Discord

Discord's own privacy policy also applies to information Discord processes on its platform.
